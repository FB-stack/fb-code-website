Where one order spends its time

Someone workingWaiting

The work is 30 min. The waiting is 1 d 5 h.

Where we would look first

  • The longest wait is 1 d, before “Approved by a manager”. Start by asking what the order is waiting for there.
  • 4 of 5 steps are done by hand, 20 h a week between them. Ask which of them only move information from one place to another.
  • One order takes 1 d 5 h, of which 30 min is work. The rest is the order sitting still.
