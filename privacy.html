What this site collects.

Short version: a contact form with a spam check, a process map that stays in your browser, no analytics, no cookies of our own, no tracking, and nothing sold to anyone.

This page describes what happens to information when you use fb-code.com. It covers the website only, not any project we run for a client under a separate agreement.

What we collect

Only one thing on this site sends us information: the contact form. If you submit it, we receive your name, email address, company, the topic you picked, and the message you wrote. There is no account to create.

The home page also lets you map a process. What you type there stays in your browser. It reaches us only if you send the contact form while the map is attached, in which case the form also carries the steps you entered, their timings, and a link that reopens the map. The contact page shows what is attached and lets you remove it before sending.

What we do not do

No analytics. No advertising or marketing pixels. No cookies — this site sets none at all. Nothing you do here is profiled, and nothing is sold, rented or shared for anyone else's marketing. Submitting the form does not add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

Three third parties are involved in running the site. Each has its own privacy terms.

  • FormspreeReceives the contact form and forwards it to us by email. Your message passes through and is stored on their systems. Their privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare TurnstileChecks that the contact form is being sent by a person and not a script. It loads only on the contact page, and only once you start filling in the form. Cloudflare then receives your IP address and details about your browser to make that decision, and runs the check in its own frame under its own terms. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • GitHub PagesHosts and serves these pages. Like any web server it records request logs, which include your IP address. GitHub's privacy statement.

Nothing else is loaded from anywhere. The typefaces, images and the site's own scripts are all served from this site, so no font service, tag manager or content network sees your visit. If you never touch the contact form, the only party that sees it is the host.

What stays on your device

If you switch the site between dark and light, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage under fbcode-theme. It never leaves your device, it is not a cookie, and it is not readable by us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

If you change the process map, your version is saved the same way under fbcode-map, so it is still there when you come back and so other pages can show it. Choosing Reset to the example on the home page deletes it.

A link copied from the map page carries the map inside the link itself, in the part after the #. Browsers do not send that part to a server, so neither we nor our host see it. Anyone you give the link to can read the map.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as we need them to reply and to keep ordinary business records. If an enquiry does not turn into a project, there is no reason for us to hold on to it — ask us to delete yours at any time and we will.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Use the contact form and say what you want done. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Who to ask

FB Code LLC runs this site and is responsible for what it collects. For anything about this page, or about information you have sent us, use the contact form.

Changes
